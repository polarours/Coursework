# Code of Conduct · 行为准则

## Our pledge (我们的承诺)

This project is a place to learn in public. We pledge to make participation
welcoming regardless of experience level, discipline, language, nationality, or
identity — and to remember that every reader is here to understand something
they did not understand yesterday.

> 这个项目是公开学习的地方。无论经验、专业、语言、国籍或身份，参与都应受到欢迎。我们始终记得：每个读者都是来弄懂昨天还不懂的东西。

## Standards (具体标准)

Behaviour that strengthens the project:

- **Show the run.** Claims about correctness or performance come with the
  command and its output. "It works on my machine" is the start of a
  conversation, not the end of one.
- **Say when you are unsure.** An honest "I could not reproduce this" is worth
  more than a confident guess.
- **Criticise the number, not the person.** A wrong result is a good thing to
  find; the person who found it is doing us a favour.
- **Assume the reader is smart and busy.** Write the thing you wish you had
  found when you were stuck at 2 a.m.

> 正确的做法：给出命令与输出；不确定就说不确定；批评数字而不是人；把读者当成聪明而忙碌的人。

Behaviour that does not belong here:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or dismissive replies to beginner questions.
- Presenting someone else's solution as the project's own work.
- Copying a course's solution key and passing it off as an independent
  implementation — the whole point of `solution/` is that it was written here.
- Publishing another person's private information.

## Scope (适用范围)

This code applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere the project's name is used to represent it.

## Enforcement (执行)

Report concerns to the maintainer at the address in `CITATION.cff`. Reports are
read, acknowledged, and acted on. Depending on severity the response is a
private word, a public correction, or removal from the project. We will not
name a reporter without their consent.

> 通过 `CITATION.cff` 中的联系方式报告。我们会阅读、回复并处理；视严重程度采取私下提醒、公开更正或移出项目。未经同意不会公开报告者身份。
