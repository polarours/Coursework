# 07 · 延伸练习 / Follow-up exercises

## 练习 1 — 最近点对（中等）
用分治法求平面最近点对，O(n log n)。 > **English:** Implement closest pair with divide and conquer.

## 练习 2 — 多边形面积（困难）
用鞋带公式计算任意多边形面积。 > **English:** Compute polygon area with the shoelace formula.

## 练习 3 — 线段交点（综合）
求两条线段交点的精确坐标（含浮点误差处理）。 > **English:** Compute exact intersection coordinates with floating-point care.

> **English:** Exercises are original variants of increasing difficulty, designed to extend the core lesson.
