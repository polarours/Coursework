# 06 · 踩坑清单 / Pitfalls

## 1. 忽略负根 / Ignoring negative roots

二次方程两个根，必须取最小的正根；负根在相机背后。

## 2. 法线未归一化 / Unnormalized normal

点积着色要求单位法线，否则亮度错误。

## 3. 背景与物体混淆 / Background vs object

必须选最近交点；否则后方球体覆盖前方。

> **English:** Three pitfalls: the quadratic has two roots so the smallest positive one must be chosen; the surface normal must be normalized before the dot product; and only the nearest intersection may determine a pixel's color, or a rear sphere would incorrectly overwrite a front one.
