# 06 · 踩坑清单 / Pitfalls

## 1. 块对齐 / Block alignment

num_sets 与 block_size 必须是 2 的幂，否则地址划分公式不成立。构造时 assert。

## 2. 命中也要更新 LRU / Update LRU on hit

命中时必须 move_to_end，否则 LRU 不正确。

## 3. 脏位与 writeback / Dirty bit

淘汰脏行必须计 writeback；否则统计数据错误。

> **English:** Three pitfalls: num_sets and block_size must be powers of two for the address formula to hold (asserted at construction); a hit must still move the line to the MRU end; and evicting a dirty line must count a writeback or the statistics will be wrong.
