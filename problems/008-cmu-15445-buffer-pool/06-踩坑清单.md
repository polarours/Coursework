# 06 · 踩坑清单 / Pitfalls

## 1. 动态属性补丁 / Dynamic attribute hacks

初版用 `Frame.index = 0` 类属性补丁，导致 LSP 报错且逻辑混乱。修复：显式 dataclass 字段。
Fix: explicit dataclass field bound at initialization.

## 2. pin 计数下溢 / pin underflow

`unpin` 必须用 `max(0, pin_count-1)`，否则重复 unpin 会变负数，破坏 victim 判断。

## 3. 淘汰时忘记清页表 / Stale page_table entry

_free_frame 必须同时 `page_table.pop` 和 `lru.pop`，否则后续 fetch 会命中失效映射。

> **English:** Three pitfalls are documented: a dynamic-attribute hack on Frame caused LSP errors and was replaced by an explicit dataclass field; unpin must clamp at zero to avoid negative pin counts; and eviction must clear both page_table and LRU, or later fetches hit a stale mapping.
